B-book commodity & index trading with an append-only, hash-chained, signed ledger.

RoleLead Engineer

WhereBitDecentro · 2026
StatusLive — demo on request
Overview

A real-money B-book trading platform for commodities and indices. Correctness is enforced structurally: every money mutation flows through a single audited choke-point into an append-only, hash-chained and signed ledger that is the source of truth.

What I built
  • Single audited choke-point for all money movement — the ledger is truth, caches only mirror it.
  • Append-only, hash-chained & signed ledger (tamper-evident; gaps detectable via monotonic sequence).
  • Per-instrument margin, liquidation and PnL.
  • Queue-batched writer keeps the hot path off the database — no data loss under load.
Architecture
  • Choke-point → durable queue (BullMQ) → batched writer
  • Hash-chained ledger + PostgreSQL of record
  • Real-time price feed feeding margin/liquidation
