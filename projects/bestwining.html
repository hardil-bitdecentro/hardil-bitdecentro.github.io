Overview

A provably-fair real-money gaming platform. Every round's outcome is independently verifiable: a per-round CSPRNG seed is generated and logged, the rules in force are frozen, and all money movement lands in a tamper-evident, hash-chained ledger.

What I built
  • Per-round CSPRNG seeds — logged, never fixed, never Date.now(); rules frozen per round.
  • Provably-fair verification any player can check.
  • Audited, hash-chained ledger for every credit and debit; single money choke-point.
  • Real-time gameplay over WebSockets; Terraform-provisioned infra on EC2.
Architecture
  • RNG fairness (seed + frozen rules) → audited ledger
  • Single money choke-point; real-time WS layer
  • Terraform + EC2 infrastructure-as-code
